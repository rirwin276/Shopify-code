Good questions

Before you start.

Is the team store really free?

Yes. Creating your store is free, with no setup fee or upfront inventory purchase. Your members pay for the gear they choose to order.

Why do I need to sign in?

Secure Shopify sign-in connects your store to your dashboard so you can manage it and return later. After signing in, you come back to the short setup form.

Do I have to collect sizes or payments?

No. Share your store's join link. Each person signs in, chooses their own size and color, and pays for their own order. We ship directly to them.

Can I change the gear later?

Yes. Your store dashboard lets you add products, choose colors, adjust logo placement, and hide items. Your store can grow with your team.

Is this just for sports teams?

Sports teams, clubs, military units, small businesses, and family groups can all create a store. The same simple setup works for each.
